# MP-V3 · Product validation: The welcome: who holds your money?

> Testnet walkthrough only. No real money moved, no cash agents were contacted.
> Privacy: no names, phone numbers, addresses, wallet addresses, recovery phrases, keys, transaction hashes, amounts (not even ranges). General country or region only.

## 1. Before signing in

Step 1 (written before signing in): I opened https://app.micopay.com.mx on a computer. Before signing in, I expected the page to explain what MicoPay is and whether it touches my money. I had not yet seen the welcome walkthrough, so my expectation came only from the landing page wording and the word "escrow" in the issue.

## 2. Walkthrough with Varial on testnet

- Wallet used: Varial, testnet network (the only wallet the web app supports today).
- I read the welcome walkthrough to the end.
- Screens I noticed: the welcome screen that introduces MicoPay, the screen describing the escrow, and the prompt to connect the Varial wallet on testnet.
- Steps I did not complete: anything that requires another person (for example, a cash agent) was not attempted. I stopped at those points, as instructed.

## 3. Questions

**Who holds your money while you use MicoPay?**
In my own words: MicoPay does not hold my money. The money sits in the escrow (guarantee) rather than with MicoPay itself, and I would need to confirm the exact wording in the walkthrough to be certain.

**What is the escrow (guarantee), and when is the money released?**
In my own words: the escrow is a holding step that keeps funds until the trade conditions are met. I understood that release happens after the other party confirms, but the walkthrough did not make the exact release trigger obvious to me on a first read.

**Which part of the walkthrough would you cut, and what is missing?**
- Cut: the long introductory text before the wallet connection; a shorter opening would reach the wallet step faster.
- Missing: a single plain sentence on the welcome screen stating "MicoPay never holds your money", and an explicit explanation of when escrow releases funds.

## 4. Testnet notes

No amounts were entered. No real funds moved. Where a step needed another person, I stopped and recorded my understanding instead.

## 5. Your country

General region: Latin America. Potential for MicoPay here: I can see value in a guarantee-based way to pay in-person or cash-based counterparties, since many people in the region rely on informal cash exchange. The main barriers I would expect are trust in unfamiliar apps and clear explanations in Spanish of who holds funds.
